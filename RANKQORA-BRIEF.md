# RankQora — Meeting Brief Template

Har client meeting se pehle ye brief Dashboard → **Brief** mein bharein.
Prices aur client list pehle se bhari hui hain. Prices 2026 ke market rates ke hisaab se rakhi gayi hain
(chhoti agency ke liye: Starter $500/month, Growth $900/month). Agar aapki prices alag hain to sirf numbers badal dein.
Agent sirf wahi bolega jo brief mein likha hai, khud se price ya result nahi banayega.

---

## Upar wale boxes

| Box | Kya likhna hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting type | Sales / discovery call | Sales call |
| Company / client | Client ke business ka naam | [Smile Dental Clinic, Toronto] |
| My role / position | Aap ka role | Founder of RankQora, Local SEO and web design agency |
| Who I am talking to | Kaun call par hai | [Clinic owner, Dr. Ahmed] |
| What a good outcome looks like | Is call ka goal | Client agrees to a free audit, then a paid Local SEO package |

## "My prepared material" box — neeche wala sab copy kar ke paste karein

```text
ABOUT RANKQORA
RankQora is a Local SEO and website design agency. We help local businesses get found on
Google Maps and Google Search and turn that traffic into calls and bookings.
We work with clients in the USA, UK, Canada and Dubai.
Services: Google Business Profile optimisation, local SEO, citation building, on-page SEO,
SEO-friendly website design, review strategy, Google Ads and Meta Ads.
Team: Nadir Ghafoor (Founder), Sumaira Gul (Co-Founder), Mudasir (Google Ads),
Mehak Malik (digital marketing), Syed Suleman (team lead), Munazza Rahman (business development).

PAST CLIENTS (say the names and the type of work; never quote numbers or results)
- The Willows, London (UK)
- Limitless Construction, Toronto (Canada)
- Private Chauffeur Dubai and Home Renovation Dubai (UAE)
- MJC Movers & Storage (USA)
- Fistfullofsudz
▶ SAY "We've worked with local businesses in London, Toronto, Dubai and the US, from construction
and home renovation to movers and chauffeur services. I can share examples of the work in the audit."
NOTE - DON'T SAY: never quote a ranking, traffic or call number for these clients.

1. INTRO / WHAT DO YOU DO?
▶ SAY
"We help local businesses show up in the Google Map pack and on Google Search when nearby
customers are searching, and we make sure the website turns those visitors into calls."

2. HOW LONG UNTIL RESULTS?
▶ SAY
"Google Business Profile fixes often show movement in the first 4 to 8 weeks. Stronger local
rankings usually build over 3 to 6 months, depending on competition in your area."
NOTE - DON'T SAY: never guarantee the number one position. Google does not allow anyone to guarantee it.

3. PRICING
▶ SAY
"We start with a free audit, so you see exactly what needs fixing before you pay anything.
After that there are three simple options. A one-time Google Business Profile setup and
optimisation is 350 dollars. Our Local SEO Starter plan is 500 dollars a month for one location:
profile management, weekly posts, citations, on-page fixes and a monthly report. The Growth plan
is 900 dollars a month and adds local content, local links, a review strategy and website fixes.
A new SEO-friendly website starts from 1,200 dollars one-time."
NOTE - DON'T SAY
My lowest monthly price is 400 dollars for the Starter plan. Only offer it if they push twice,
and only with a 3-month commitment. Google Ads or Meta Ads management starts at 300 dollars a
month plus their ad budget. For UK clients quote the same numbers in pounds; for Dubai, in dollars.

4. "CAN YOU GUARANTEE NUMBER ONE ON GOOGLE?"
▶ SAY
"Honestly, nobody can guarantee a fixed position, and anyone who does is a red flag. What we
guarantee is the work: a fully optimised profile, clean citations, monthly reports, and clear
progress you can track in calls, direction requests and rankings."

5. "WE TRIED SEO BEFORE AND IT DIDN'T WORK"
▶ SAY
"That's common. Usually the profile categories, reviews or local signals were never fixed
properly. We start with an audit and show you exactly what was missed before you pay for anything."

6. REPORTING
▶ SAY
"You get a simple monthly report: rankings in your area, calls and direction requests from
your Google profile, and what we did that month."

7. CONTRACT
▶ SAY
"The first commitment is 3 months, because local SEO needs that long to show real movement.
After that it's month-to-month, and you can stop any time.

8. NEXT STEP / CLOSE
▶ SAY
"The best next step is a free audit of your Google profile and website. I can send it within
2-3 days, and then we decide together what to fix first."

NOTE - DON'T SAY: never promise fake reviews, review gating, or keyword-stuffed business names.
These break Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.
```

## Tips

- **Ask Claude what else it needs** zaroor dabayein. Woh 4-6 sawal poochega; jawab dene se live answers bohat behtar ho jaate hain.
- Har client ke liye brief thoda badlein: shehar, business type, aur unka main problem.
- Call ke baad **Notes & summary → Generate summary** dabayein. Summary, action items, follow-up message aur
  **Client roadmap** (phase by phase plan) ban jayega. **Download Word (.docx)** se file client ko bhej sakte hain.
